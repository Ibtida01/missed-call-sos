Missed Call SOS

Register a location

Do this once, now, while there's data and a working phone. During an actual emergency this page isn't needed — just call the number and hang up, same as always.

Registering for someone else? Stand at their home, enter their phone number below, and use your own phone's GPS. One volunteer with one smartphone can register an entire neighbourhood this way — the number and the device don't need to match.

Why we ask: if a real emergency call ever comes from this number, the coordinator's map will show your actual location instead of a rough estimate. This page uses your phone's built-in GPS (free, no app, nothing installed) — it only runs once, right now, not during the emergency call itself.
